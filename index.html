Profile

How I work

I supervise ICT for the WAGL OML 11 JV under ND Engineering (Sahara Group), with dual support covering Arahas and SGIR Rigs. My day runs on the Microsoft Power Platform — Power Automate flows, Power Apps and SharePoint for JV workflows and reporting — alongside wide-area point-to-point WAN administration and remote LiFePO4 inverter monitoring with live cell/load/grid telemetry. When rigs move, I handle the IT decommissioning and recommissioning that keeps them productive at the new site.

The through-line of my career is making operations run on solid technology: 5,000+ machines migrated to the EMG–Seplat JV platform with zero downtime, 35,000 BOPD of production supported at OML 42, Starlink and 4G networks deployed across field locations, CCTV & biometric access control, and dependable backup power. Alongside the field work I build software — OML11 AssetHub and the OML 11 Process Safety Tracker are live production apps used in the JV, developed with AI-agent tooling that lets one engineer deliver like a small team.

I work where the assets are — offshore vessels, gas terminals, flow stations and rigs (BOSIET-certified) — and I document everything: audit trails, timelines and reports that give JV partners confidence.
